Hi, I'm Luciano. I am a chemical engineer and physics and chemistry teacher based in Saladillo, Buenos Aires, Argentina. I'm also a father. I am interested in the natural sciences, technology and education.

This site brings together my educational resources and projects applying technology to environmental monitoring, scientific simulation and territorial management. It is a place to share tools, explore ideas and connect teaching with the problems I work on.

## What I do

I teach science and develop [teaching notes, activities and digital resources](/notes/) in Spanish. I want students to explore concepts through experiments, data and simulations. I also maintain a [Physics concept map](/conceptos/), in Spanish, to explore connections between topics.

My projects involve sensors, environmental data, web mapping and artificial intelligence. [Aire Escolar](/en/projects/aq-saladillo/) and [EMAS](/en/projects/ema-saladillo/) make environmental information accessible; [Thermal Beings](/en/projects/seres-termicos/) explores models through simulation; [SIG Vial PBA](/en/projects/dvba-gis/) connects territorial information with road management.

My professional experience includes laboratory coordination, quality control, water analysis, research and development, industrial inspection and environmental management. I have also worked with ISO/IEC 17025 and ISO 9001 management systems and technical support for production.

## Interests

I am interested in air quality, the natural sciences, and ways to use technology to learn and solve problems. I enjoy building and experimenting with DIY and IoT projects.

I support free software and sharing knowledge. I value the strategic and responsible use of new technologies, including artificial intelligence, in education, research and professional work.

Outside work, I enjoy swimming and listening to music from the 1970s and 1980s. I am also interested in healthy eating.
