Inspired by “Los seres térmicos”, by **Jorge Luis Borges and Margarita Guerrero**, this narrative brings together literature, physics and simulation. Each chapter poses a question, explains its model and offers an interactive scene to explore it.

**Start with the [prologue in Spanish](/projects/simus/bestiario/prologo/)** and follow the previous/next links. The journey moves from two bodies with uniform temperatures to fields, patterns and articulated individuals. Appendices provide the physical background and technical prototypes.

The narrative, equations and simulation controls are currently in Spanish. This page introduces the project in English; it does not present the chapters as translated.

The simulations are computational models and fictions: a pattern does not demonstrate biological life, and a programmed behaviour does not follow from diffusion alone.
